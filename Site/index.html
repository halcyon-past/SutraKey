Engineered for Craft, Silence & Speed

Zero telemetry, zero dock pollution, and zero latency. Built with Swift 6 strict concurrency and Apple Human Interface Guidelines.

Zero Modifier Pollution Guard

Using bitwise masks on deviceIndependentFlagsMask, SutraKey only triggers when Shift is pressed alone. Pressing ⌘ Command, ⌥ Option, or ⌃ Control immediately dismisses or prevents the wheel.

Ephemeral Menu Bar Popover

SutraKey is 100% invisible when idle (LSUIElement = YES). The menu bar icon only appears while conversion jobs are running, with live speeds, ETAs, and a 1.5s auto-dismiss grace period.

Frosted Glass Toast Panel

Custom floating SutraToastPanel rendered in frosted .ultraThinMaterial with native "Show in Finder" and "Copy Error Log" action buttons.

Strict Sandboxed Output

All downloads route strictly to ~/Downloads with traversal defense against ../ and automatic title sanitization with --no-exec and --restrict-filenames.

Offscreen Web-to-PDF Engine

Offscreen WKWebView strips intrusive ads, navigation bars, and cookie banners to export beautiful, distraction-free reader PDFs.

Binary Bootstrap & Self-Updater

Auto-locates yt-dlp and ffmpeg across bundled resources, ~/.local/share/sutrakey, and Homebrew paths, with built-in one-click self-updating.
